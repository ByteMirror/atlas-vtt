## New

**Physical Dice**
- Roll real 3D dice on your map. Switch the dice panel from RNG to Physical, pick your dice and press Roll, then drag a die to throw it or press Throw. The result appears like any other roll, with modifiers.
- Statblock dice, the roll log and hit points without placed tokens roll physical dice too when Physical is on.
- Dress your dice with dice packs: import a pack folder in the asset manager's new Dice tab and choose it for the collection.
- Tune how the dice look and read in Collection Settings → Physical Dice: pack, size, shadows, light, how long they settle and how squarely a face must land.

## Fixed

- A formula like `d6+2d8` no longer adds the 2 as a modifier.
